Grandma's Till

Two screens, one database. Open each on its own device: an order placed on one shows up on the other within a second.

For Grandma Counter Ring up sales, work through the order queue, take payment and check today's takings. For customers Order here Browse the menu, order, pay by card or at the counter, and watch for your number.

Demo data: the menu, prices, six weeks of sales history and the first 40 members are simulated. Orders placed on either screen are saved to the database.
