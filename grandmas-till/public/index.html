        The four models
        Each one is a few lines of arithmetic, so anyone at the shop can check why it said what it said.
        
      
        
        
        ModelHow it worksShows up as
        
        
          
        Demand forecastAverage of the last six same weekdays, newest counted most, nudged by the change between the last two fortnights. Scored every day against what really sold.Bake sheet, demand chart
        
          
        Basket liftHow much more often two items share a sale than chance would predict.Goes-well-with prompt, combo ideas
        
          
        Taste matchA member's purchases become a seven-note taste profile, recent visits weighted more. Items are ranked by how well they match what sets that member apart from the average customer.Usual and try-next, Fall parfait test
        
          
        Flavor trendEach flavor note's share of items sold in the last 14 days, against the 14 days before.Flavor trends
